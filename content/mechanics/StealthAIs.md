## Overview

For a stealth game I am working on, I worked on the AIs, using Unreal engine along with free animations I found online at mixamo. The features for these enemies include: patrolling along their personal spline path (can be disabled in their settings); state switching between passive, investigating and aggressive; sight bars that increase as long as they see the player before switching to aggresive; hearing that causes them to patrol the area they heard a noise.
