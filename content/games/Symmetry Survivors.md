## Overview

This small game was made for my first game jam, inspired by 'Vampire Survivors' its a survival based game where enemies will constantly attack you to overwhelm you, but during the night, the tide turns and you become the hunter. The game jam was one and a half days and it was the first time our team used Godot game engine.
