## Overview
OverWorked is a short game where you, a new hire for a game company, need to pump out as many games as you can within your deadline. You are tasked with making the game entirely on your own and you best not fail or you're fired. 

This is project that one of my designer friends was doing for one of their submissions. I made the general gameplay loop for the game and all the code involved with that.
