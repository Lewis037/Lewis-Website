## Overview

Kagemusha is a first person stealth game set in a fictional version of edo period Japan. You are a ninja who has been tasked with assassinating a strange war general that resides in the mountains, but things don't go as planned as mysterious powers wielded by the target catch you off guard and defeat you. With the help of mysterious beings, embark on a journey to get your revenge and stop this madman, honing your techniques and learning more about this new power.
